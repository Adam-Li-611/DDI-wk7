▼ related-party operating arrangement (hotel only)

Sonesta Management Agreement (Hotel)

GPIT owns 20 Mass Ave NW, DC (Royal Sonesta Capitol Hill, 247 rooms) → leases hotel to 20 Mass Ave TRS Inc. (taxable REIT sub) → TRS contracts with Sonesta DC Hotel LLC to operate.

Related party: Adam Portnoy is director & controlling shareholder of Sonesta; Jennifer Clark is a Sonesta director. Fees: 1.5% / 3.0% base mgmt; 20% incentive over owner’s priority; 1.75% / 3.5% brand promotion; loyalty fee.

External Manager (RMR) OPI / Officers Board Independent / Special Committee Subsidiary Board (silo) Related-party agreement
Key takeaways for the memo: (1) OPI is a fully externally-managed REIT — every officer except the CRO is on RMR’s payroll, and conflicts run through the Independent Trustees / Compensation Committee. (2) Pre-petition, the Board layered in three independent fiduciaries (Pohl at OPI; Frizzley for the 2027 silo; Bartels for the 2029 silo) so each major creditor silo has a clean, conflicts-cleansed decision-maker on restructuring and on potential claims investigation. (3) The Sonesta hotel arrangement is a related-party fee stream sitting at the GPIT/TRS level and worth flagging in the governance discussion.
