Pre & Post-petition

Direct-debt reconciliation: $1,819mm (OPI) + $425mm (OPI WF Borrower) + $54.3mm (UBS Borrowers) + $123.0mm (JPM Borrowers) = $2,421.3mm total Company funded debt. Of which Debtors: $1,819 + $425 = $2,244mm (~92.7%); Non-Debtors: $177.3mm (~7.3%).

Q3 takeaways: ~90% of entities are Debtors (73 of 81 — 73 Debtors + 7 Non-Debtor CMBS SPEs + 1 Non-Debtor JV). Only 7 wholly-owned properties (plus 2 JV properties) sit at non-Debtors, holding ~7.3% of consolidated funded debt — non-recourse to Debtors except OPI's limited carve-out guaranty.
