Guarantee Architecture & Key Takeaways
  • Silos do not cross-guarantee each other’s 1L debt — each silo has its own sub-guarantor set, own independent director, own restructuring advisors (Paul Weiss/Ducera at M29; White & Case/Houlihan at S29; Milbank/Evercore at M27; Glenn Agre/Province at PGN/Unencumbered)
  • Layered 2L liens create a clean waterfall on the overlapping pools: RC (1L) → S29 (2L on RC’s 19 props, 1L on its own 19) → M27 (2L on S29’s 19, 1L on its own 35)
  • The 31-sub unsecured package is the bridge that makes M27 and PGN economically equivalent at those subs and pushes Senior Unsecured even further down the structural ladder
  • Cash management: GPIT Concentration Account is the group hub; Credit Facility silo is self-contained (DACAs to RC agent — skipped from the OPI sweep); each post-petition silo runs its own operating account, generating new IC claims
  • Independent directors and silo-specific advisors signal silo integrity — the estates are intended to be administered separately rather than substantively consolidated
